About Whiskers & Coffee

A little café built on a big love for cats, good coffee and cosy afternoons.

Our History

Whiskers & Coffee began in 2020 when two friends, both lifelong cat lovers, dreamed of a calm place in Singapore where people could unwind with a good drink and a friendly cat.

We opened our doors in 2021 with four rescue cats and a small coffee bar. Today we welcome students, families and friends every day, and we have helped many of our cats find loving forever homes.

Our Mission

To create a warm and welcoming café where every guest can relax with quality drinks and desserts while caring cats enjoy a safe, happy home.

Our Vision

To be Singapore's most loved cat café, known for great coffee, happy cats and a community that supports animal welfare and adoption.

Our Values

The promises we keep to our guests and our cats.
