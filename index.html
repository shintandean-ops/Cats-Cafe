Welcome to Whiskers & Coffee

Whiskers & Coffee is a cosy cat café for cat lovers, students, families and friends. We serve freshly brewed coffee, handmade cakes and waffles in a warm space shared with our gentle resident cats.

Come in, slow down and let a purring friend keep you company.
